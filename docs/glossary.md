# Glossary

**Status:** Approved

## Roles

| Term | Meaning | Votes | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Assembly | The delegates in session. It holds the authority. | Through its delegates | Reglamento 05-00 |
| Delegate | A person who votes on ballots. | Yes | Reglamento 02-07 01, 05-04 |
| Presiding Officer | The regional secretary, who represents the Conferencia General (Asociación General) and presides over the assembly. Screens call this person «Secretario regional». | Only to break a persistent tie. From the 4th vote after a tie, the mesa can add them as one more secret voter, and they count in the base of those votes. | Reglamento 05-09 01, 05-09 02. `docs/scope.md` rule 17. Reglamento 02-07 01 also lists this person as a delegate, and this project treats the tie-break vote as their only vote. |
| Regional Secretary | See Presiding Officer. | | |
| Temporary Secretary | The delegate elected from those present, at the beginning of the assembly, to serve as its secretary. | Yes, as a delegate | Reglamento 05-10 01, 05-10 02, 05-11 01 |
| Union Secretary | The officer who issues credentials, calls the roll and counts for quorum. | Yes, as an ex officio delegate | Reglamento 05-01 03, 05-01 07, 05-04 01, 05-04 04 |
| Presiding Board | The Presiding Officer and the Temporary Secretary, who instruct the operator. | According to each member's role | Project term |
| Operator | The technician who runs the tool on the Presiding Board's instructions. The operator has no voice in the assembly. | No | Project term |
